Privacy Policy

TFT Worldwide Express & Logistics — Laiba WhatsApp assistant
Last updated: 3 September 2026 · Contact: rabbanalidesigner@gmail.com

This policy explains how TFT Worldwide Express & Logistics (“TFT”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you chat with Laiba, our shipping assistant on WhatsApp or the FreightOS demo.

1. Who we are

TFT provides international courier and logistics quotations and related customer support. Laiba helps you get shipping rates and share details needed for a quotation or booking follow-up.

2. What we collect

When you message Laiba, we may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Messages you send (destination, weight, goods, packing, city, name if you share it)
  • Language preference inferred from your messages
  • Technical delivery data from Meta WhatsApp (message IDs, timestamps)

We do not ask for payment card details in the chat.

3. Why we use your data

  • To reply to your messages and provide shipping quotations
  • To save lead details for our sales desk so a human can follow up
  • To improve accuracy of rates and conversation quality
  • To comply with Meta WhatsApp and applicable law

4. Legal basis

We process chat data because you contacted us and asked for a shipping quote or support (pre-contract / legitimate interest), and because Meta requires us to handle WhatsApp messages responsibly.

5. Sharing

Messages are delivered through Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Our systems (including hosting on our servers) store lead fields so staff can help you. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention

Lead records are kept only as long as needed for sales follow-up and operations (typically up to 12 months unless a longer period is required by law). You may ask us to delete your chat/lead data.

7. Your choices

  • Reply STOP to opt out of further automated messages where supported
  • Email us to access, correct, or delete your lead data
  • Ask to speak with a human agent during the chat

8. Children

Laiba is for business shipping inquiries and is not directed at children under 16.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and Meta’s platform security features. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed on servers outside your country (including our hosting provider and Meta). We take reasonable steps to protect it.

11. Contact / data deletion

For privacy or deletion requests, email rabbanalidesigner@gmail.com with your WhatsApp number and a short description of your request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
